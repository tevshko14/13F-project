🐼 Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

PaperPanda ("we", "us", or "our") is a financial data platform that helps investors track institutional holdings, insider trades, congressional trades, analyst ratings, and market signals. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

Account information — When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google. We store your email to identify your account and manage your subscription status.

Usage data — We may collect anonymised information about how you use PaperPanda (pages visited, features used) to improve the product. We do not fingerprint individual users or sell behavioural data.

Payment information — If you choose to support PaperPanda, payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. We only receive a confirmation of payment and your email to record your supporter status.

Communications — If you contact us via the support page or email, we retain that correspondence to respond to you.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and maintain your session
  • To track your supporter / subscription status
  • To send product notifications you have opted into
  • To improve PaperPanda's features and fix bugs
  • To respond to support requests

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

PaperPanda uses the following third-party services, each of which has its own privacy policy:

  • Clerk — authentication and session management (clerk.com/privacy)
  • Google OAuth — sign-in via Google account (policies.google.com/privacy)
  • Supabase — database and backend infrastructure (supabase.com/privacy)
  • Stripe — payment processing (stripe.com/privacy)
  • Railway — server hosting (railway.app/legal/privacy)

4. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect or store passwords (handled entirely by Clerk / Google)
  • We do not collect your brokerage account or portfolio information
  • We do not use advertising trackers or sell data to data brokers
  • We do not collect sensitive financial data beyond payment confirmation

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you request deletion, we will remove your account and associated personal data within 30 days. Anonymised, aggregated usage data may be retained indefinitely.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location you may have rights including:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Portability — receive your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

7. Cookies

PaperPanda uses strictly necessary cookies for authentication (session tokens managed by Clerk) and local storage to remember your theme preference (dark / light mode) and notification read state. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies.

8. Children's Privacy

PaperPanda is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will note the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of PaperPanda after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact us at:

Email: privacy@paperpanda.io
Website: paperpanda.io
